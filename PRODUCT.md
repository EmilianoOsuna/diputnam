# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + Lenis + GSAP for the MVP. Content remains local until Sanity is implemented in a later phase.

## Users

Buyers and investors evaluating residential real-estate projects from Putnam.

## Product Purpose

Putnam develops residential real-estate projects in Bolivia and helps buyers and investors understand the opportunity, the project, and the company behind it. The website succeeds when visitors quickly understand what Putnam does and can continue toward a project inquiry.

## Positioning

Putnam integrates design, planning, construction execution, and long-term patrimonial thinking in one residential development approach.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive to evaluate a residential project, understand Putnam's credibility, review the featured development, and decide whether to request more information or begin a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first implementation covers only the home route.
- Future pages will be added through separate OpenSpec changes.
- Sanity is intentionally deferred from the MVP.
- The current Putnam content, colors, and logo must remain available as source material.
- The experience must work on desktop and mobile and preserve access to content with reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the Putnam name, current colors, and current logo.
- Use `https://dsgninterior.se/en` as the binding visual reference for the home composition, hero, typography, layout, and animation behavior.
- Adapt the reference to Putnam's own content, imagery, and brand identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing content and brand implementation in `index.html`.
- Putnam and Ereditá content currently present in the repository.
- `Inspo.md` with visual references and motion requirements.
- No approved Sanity dataset or production CMS content yet.

## Product Principles

- Make the residential opportunity understandable quickly.
- Show credibility through specific project and company information.
- Treat design and execution as one proposition.
- Keep the experience visually precise without hiding essential information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must remain readable, navigable, and complete when motion is reduced and on narrow mobile screens.
